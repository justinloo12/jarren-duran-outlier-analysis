- Duran signed a **1-year, $7.7M** deal for 2026, avoiding arbitration; the club **declined** an $8M option — a signal in itself. Duran is *not* in that bucket — his contract is short and cheap — but it shows how quickly a Red Sox OF bat can go from asset to liability, which argues for selling Duran while he still has positive, controllable value rather than holding into decline.
